ABOUT

Experience

2024.4 — Present
Researcher, Huawei Hong Kong Research Center

My work focuses on reward modeling for code LLMs and coding agents, including training scalar reward models based on the Bradley–Terry model for code training data selection, and developing generalist reward models for coding-agent verification.

Education

2016.9 — 2024.1
Ph.D. in Computer Science, The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology

Under the supervision of Prof. Shing-Chi Cheung, my PhD research focused on Android hybrid programming, including empirical studies of cross-language bugs induced by WebViews and the development of bug detection techniques based on static and dynamic program analysis.

2012.9 — 2016.7
B.Eng. in Software Engineering, Yangzhou University

Under the supervision of Prof. Xiaobing Sun, my undergraduate research focused on software evolution and comprehension, leveraging unsupervised NLP techniques based on topic modeling to uncover patterns in software development histories.

Research interests

Reward Models · Post-Training · Coding LLMs and Agents · Data Curation · Software Engineering
